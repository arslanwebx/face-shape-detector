## 4. Content Writing Checklist

Apply this checklist directly to every final, publish-ready article on this site — not as notes or a draft outline. The goal is useful, original, well-structured content that satisfies search intent and follows strong content, on-page SEO, and technical SEO practices.

**Before writing:**

- Review existing site pages, related articles, the target keyword, search intent, internal-linking opportunities, and current site structure.
- Do not duplicate existing content, compete with another page targeting the same intent, or add sections only to inflate word count.
- Research the topic thoroughly and verify important facts using current, credible, authoritative sources. Never invent statistics, claims, quotes, examples, or expert opinions.

**Structure & intent:**

- Answer the reader's main question in 2–3 lines as the first paragraph, then expand with explanation, steps, examples, and supporting detail under H2/H3 headings.
- Match the real search intent — understand exactly what the reader wants to know, solve, compare, or decide.
- Use one clear H1 and a logical H2/H3 hierarchy. Never skip heading levels or use headings purely for visual styling.
- Make every article structurally unique: vary the introduction, heading order, examples, paragraph flow, comparisons, FAQs, and conclusion.

**Tone & voice:**

- Keep content informational and useful. Avoid sales language, product hype, affiliate-style filler, exaggerated claims, and unnecessary promotion.
- Write for readers first, search engines second — SEO should improve clarity, not distort natural language.
- Write in a natural, expert voice for the niche. Use active voice, clear wording, human sentence patterns. Avoid robotic transitions and long dashes.
- Make it engaging through clear storytelling, light and respectful humor when appropriate, relatable examples, varied sentence structure, and smooth transitions — without sacrificing accuracy or depth.

**Keywords & SEO:**

- Use the primary keyword naturally in the title, introduction, a relevant heading, and throughout the content where it fits.
- Include related keywords, entities, subtopics, synonyms, and common questions naturally — never force or mechanically repeat them.
- Create a unique SEO title and meta description matching search intent, including the main keyword naturally, with no duplication from another page.
- Use a short, readable, keyword-relevant URL slug. Don't change an existing published URL unless necessary; add a proper 301 redirect if it must change.
- Prevent keyword cannibalization: check existing pages before publishing. Each page must target a distinct primary keyword and a clearly different search intent.

**Depth & formatting:**

- Cover the topic completely but stay lean — remove repetition, vague claims, irrelevant background, and unnecessary sections.
- Choose word count based on topic and intent — write enough to fully answer the subject, no filler.
- Explain technical terms simply, and add examples, comparisons, common mistakes, limitations, and expert tips where relevant.
- Keep content scannable: clear headings, short (max 3-line) paragraphs, selective bold text, and lists only when they aid readability.
- Use tables only when they make comparisons, prices, features, data, or choices easier to understand.
- Outperform competing content through clarity, deeper explanations, original examples, practical advice, and more complete coverage.

**E-E-A-T & trust:**

- Support important claims with 2–3 natural external links to trustworthy sources (official docs, government sites, research, recognized organizations).
- Keep information accurate, specific, balanced, and properly attributed. Avoid overpromising; note when professional help may be needed.
- Add relevant internal links naturally to useful tools, guides, category pages, or related articles, using descriptive anchor text.

**FAQ & images:**

- Include a genuine FAQ section with useful follow-up questions, plus valid JSON-LD FAQ schema that exactly matches the visible FAQs.
- Create one unique featured image and 2–3 original in-content images only when they genuinely improve the article. Optimize file size, dimensions, filenames, and alt text.

**Technical & pre-publish checks:**

- Confirm the correct canonical URL, sitemap inclusion, no accidental robots.txt/noindex blocking, proper mobile rendering, and no broken links.
- Before publishing, check originality, plagiarism, grammar, punctuation, heading hierarchy, keyword use, links, factual accuracy, image optimization, and overall readability.
- Reference for understanding original-publishing value (internal use only, never cite or mention in content): Google patent US8707459B2 — https://patents.google.com/patent/US8707459B2/en
